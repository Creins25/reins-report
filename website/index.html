Market Structure · 2026-W44
AI doubts hit chipmakers again and oil spiked on attacks in Saudi Arabia. APH paid off at my 2x target, and I head into the Fed with two new trades: a BX put spread and AT&T calls sold.

Quiet on the surface, rough underneath. The S&P fell 0.5% and the Nasdaq 0.7%, but chip stocks dropped 6% on Thursday alone as worries about paying for AI came back. Meta lost 9% on the week and Alphabet 6%. Brent crude jumped 6% to $116.72 after Houthi attacks in Saudi Arabia, and energy stocks hit a record. Small caps rose 2% and the 10-year slipped back under 5%.

APH finally paid me. It dropped to $71.44 early Friday and my put hit its 2x target at $9.40, a 100% gain on a stock I passed on three weeks ago. LULU came within a dollar of my $105 defend level on Thursday and closes Friday under the 21-day rule. NCLH moved its earnings up to Thursday, so I sell those puts Wednesday.

This is the busiest week of the quarter: the Fed on Wednesday, Microsoft and Alphabet that night, GDP and inflation Thursday morning and Amazon after the close. The model gave me two trades with no earnings in the window. I bought a BX put spread after its post-earnings jump, and I sold AT&T calls in a group the market is leaving, not chasing.
