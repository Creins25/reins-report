Two weeks in and basically flat. One big winner I haven't booked is the only thing offsetting two oversized losers. The real lesson isn't stock picking, it's sizing. Fixing that now.

Two weeks into running this thing live and the scoreboard is humbling, not because the picks were bad, but because I sized them backwards. The market itself did exactly what a healthy tape does: VIX collapsed from 21-plus midweek back to 17.7, small caps ripped (IWM +4%), and money rotated straight back into beta after the early-June wobble. Every short-vol trade on the screen printed money this week. The problem is I wasn't positioned for it. I had a big bear put on Clorox right as staples caught a bid (+2.85%), and that single oversized loser is most of the damage.

Here's the honest read on my own book: on closed trades I'm down about 7.5% on the account. The only reason the whole thing is sitting near breakeven is CNC, my health-care bull call, up 68% on the spread and still open. So the lesson of these two weeks isn't "pick better." META and CNC prove the picking is fine. It's "size right." I bet the most money on my two worst ideas (CLX and MO) and the least on my best. Good selection plus backwards sizing equals a flat fortnight. That's a math problem, not a market problem, and it's the thing I'm fixing this week.

The regime itself is still tech-led but breadth is finally widening, which is good for stock-picking. The last six months everything moved together and now dispersion is back. VIX in the 17 handle is too cheap to short index vol and rich enough on individual names to keep selling premium where the IV is mispriced. FOMC is Wednesday (June 17). I'm not betting on the decision (a hold is basically priced), but Powell's tone and the dot-plot can move the tape, so I'm carrying a mildly bearish net book into it: one long (CNC) against two defined-risk shorts (BX, SMCI). If the rally keeps grinding I lose a little on the shorts and CNC carries me; if FOMC cracks it, the bears pay. Either way I'm done being the guy whose biggest position is his worst idea.
