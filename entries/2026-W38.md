Risk came off almost everywhere. SPY fell 0.77%, small caps took the worst of it at -2.4%, and the VIX jumped 9%. Health care was the ugliest sector at -3.6%, materials -2.8%. Energy led at +1.7% and is now up 42.7% on the year. Only tech and communications were also green, both by less than a point. TLT fell another 1.6% as long yields kept climbing.

Both of my longs stopped out. FCX broke below the 75 strike and closed at -50%. TGT fell 5.2% in a week and hit its stop too. That was the trade I flagged last Sunday for having a 36% probability of profit, and the warning played out exactly. Together they cost 8.6% of the account. The two shorts paid for most of it: ZTS is up 50% and NCLH up 28%, so the book is roughly flat in dollars on the week.

The model produced no directional picks at all. Ninety-six longs and eighty-five shorts cleared the score filter, seventy and fifty-seven survived factor alignment, and then every one of them failed the last gate of two factors at 72 or better. That is the system working, not breaking.

The insider factor is finally alive. Form 4 data had not been pulled since May 30, so the factor scored a flat 50 for three months. A full EDGAR run loaded 6,403 buy and sell transactions inside the 90-day window and flagged 8 cluster buys. It caught something immediately: nearly every top long showed insider selling, including TGT at seven out of ten confidence.

With no directional signal I sold premium instead. PLTR made its 2026 high on August 31 and has given back 10.3%, so I sold the 185 call right at the level that failed. NRG is a separate bet, not a second AI trade, and I checked that: the two are negatively correlated at -0.41. Skipped ABNB and the WMT condor because both had strikes under 6% out of the money.
