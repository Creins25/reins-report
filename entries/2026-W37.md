## Narrative

The index went nowhere and that hid a lot. SPY finished up 0.11% with the VIX at 14.5, which looks like a week where nothing happened. Underneath, energy ripped another 2.2% and is now up 40.3% on the year, by far the best sector in the market. Everything cyclical and rate sensitive went the other way: discretionary -2.0%, materials -1.4%, real estate -1.2%, industrials -1.1%, staples -1.0%. Tech and utilities were the only other groups green.

The reason showed up Friday. August payrolls came in at 162,000 against an estimate near 55,000, roughly three times what was expected. Good news was bad news. That print took a September rate cut off the table and put a hike on it, with odds of a hike at the September 16 meeting jumping to 60% from 49% the day before. Trump then told the Fed and Chair Warsh to cut anyway and threatened to stop trade with countries the US runs a deficit with. The market ignored him and priced the opposite. The front end sold off while the long end held, so the curve flattened and TLT finished the week down 0.8%.

On the book, ZTS is finally working. The stock is at $75.81 against my $77.73 entry, so the 80/70 bear put is in the money and green. FCX is not. Copper cooled and the stock sits at $72.73, below my $75 long strike, so that bull call spread is underwater with three weeks to the force-close on September 25. I am holding both to the force-close rather than guessing.

Two new picks. TGT scored a perfect 100, with momentum at the 96th percentile and estimate revisions at the 92nd, so I am taking the 165/180 bull call. I want to be honest about the part I do not love: the model's own probability of profit is 36%, and Half-Kelly says 2% sizing while the tier rule put it at 9.5%.
