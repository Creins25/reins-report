Intel fell 29% on Friday on a weak forecast. The S&P lost 0.6% on the week and the Nasdaq 2%, closing its worst month since March 2025. The Fed held rates, but two members voted for a hike, and the market now expects one in December.

Here is the number I cannot ignore: the eight call spreads I have sold and closed lost about 8% of the account this year, while everything else made about 18%. So this week I took one trade, a Boeing put spread, and passed on three more sold calls. Apple reports Monday, the midterms are Tuesday and jobs come Friday.
